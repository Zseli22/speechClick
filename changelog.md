# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-30

First public release.

* Replaces speech with a short click when a new utterance is similar to the previous one and arrives within the configured interval.
* Leaves different text alone, no matter how quickly it follows.
* Toggle with `NVDA+control+shift+s`.
* Settings panel with an enable checkbox, the minimum interval between similar utterances, and the resume delay.
