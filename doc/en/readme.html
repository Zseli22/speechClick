speechClick

speechClick is a small NVDA add-on that keeps fast scrolling from turning speech into noise.

When NVDA is asked to speak a string that is very similar to the one it spoke a moment ago, speechClick plays a short click instead of the speech. Text that differs meaningfully is always spoken in full, however quickly it follows.

Requirements

NVDA 2023.1 or later. Tested with NVDA 2023.3 and 2026.3.

Usage

The add-on needs no configuration. It starts working as soon as it is installed.

CommandDescription
NVDA+control+shift+sToggles speechClick on and off

The command can be changed in NVDA's Input Gestures dialog, under the "Speech Click" category.

Settings

Open NVDA menu, Preferences, Settings, Speech Click.

SettingDefaultRange
Enable speech click filteronon or off
Minimum time between repeated speech (ms)501 to 5000
Speech resume delay after scrolling stops (ms)10000 to 30000

A click replaces speech only when three things are true: the new utterance is at least 85 percent similar to the previous one, it arrives sooner than the minimum time, and it arrives sooner than the resume delay. In practice the smaller of those two values is the limit.

  • Minimum time between repeated speech — how close together two similar utterances must be before the second one becomes a click. Raise it to click more often, lower it to hear more of what is scrolling past.
  • Speech resume delay — an upper bound on how long after the previous utterance a click may still be used, so that the click does not linger after you stop scrolling.

The similarity threshold is fixed at 85 percent and is not exposed in the settings.

How it works

speechClick wraps NVDA's speech function. For each utterance it joins the text, compares it with the previous utterance using Python's difflib sequence matching, and checks the elapsed time. If the conditions hold it plays click.wav and returns without speaking; otherwise it calls the original function untouched.
